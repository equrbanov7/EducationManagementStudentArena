Read-only audit. No source file was edited; this document is the only output.

## 0. How to read this

**Target design system** = `static/css/design-tokens.css`, `static/css/ems_components.css`, `static/css/ems_ui/*.css`,
`templates/partials/ems_ui/*` (`_content_header`, `_kpi_row`, `_filter_bar`, `_data_table`, `_empty`, `_drawer`, `_dialog`,
`_form_dialog`, `_reason_dialog`, `_banner`, `_tabs`, `_stepper`, `_status_badge` …), status badges from `core/ui/status_catalog.py`,
styled select `data-bootstrap-select`.

**Three generations exist in the code today** (this is the key to the ranking):

| Gen | What it looks like | Where |
|---|---|---|
| G1 — Bootstrap-default | `btn btn-*`, `card-header/card-body`, `alert alert-*`, `badge bg-*`, `table`, `accordion-item`, `modal fade`, `form-control`; page-local colour palette (`--primary-blue`, `--light-blue`, hex) | labs, course sections/modals, exam result/history, teacher grading, superadmin, posts/blog |
| G2 — 2026-09 bespoke "modern" | own classes (`modern-*`, `section-card`, `sjx-*`, `sd-*`, `fxc-*`, `syl-*`), token-ish CSS but hand-rolled tabs/search/KPI/tables, no `ems_ui` partials | student cabinet sections, student journal, exam centre, exam statistics, question-submission pages, syllabus |
| G3 — current (`ems_ui`) | `_content_header` + `_kpi_row` + `_filter_bar` + `_data_table` + `_drawer`/`_dialog` + `status_catalog` | groups registry, workload, system settings, journal list, my-exams list, org/RİM/people/HR sections, question-bank list, audit, announcements, timetable generator |

G3 covers most of the staff cabinet already. The work that is left is **(a) standalone pages reached from the cabinet** (exams, courses, labs)
and **(b) a handful of G2 student sections**. Hard-coded hex and legacy palettes are concentrated in `apps/exams/static/exams/css/`,
`apps/registrar/static/registrar/css/` and `apps/labs/`, not in the cabinet CSS.

**Evidence marks**: **V** = opened live on the QA clone (`localhost:8011`, `qa.ikt_rehber`); **S** = static analysis only (student, lab and course pages cannot be
opened with the available account — no student/teacher login was created, no qa.* password touched). Counts are from grep over the template/CSS files named.

**Audience estimate** (clone Statistics screen, same order as prod): ≈ 8.6 k students, ≈ 750 teachers, ≈ 260 staff. "Frequency" is my estimate, not telemetry.

## 1. Headline findings

1. The cabinet shell and almost every staff section already render on G3 — I opened ~25 of them (list in section 5) and none looked old. The "old" feel is on
   **pages that leave the cabinet** (own header, own back button, Bootstrap cards) and on **student-facing result/journal screens**.
2. Several modern-looking pages are **re-skins through override layers**, not rewrites: `jd2_theme.css` over `jd2.css` (182 hex), `teacher_exam_detail_theme.css` +
   `teacher_surfaces_theme.css` over the exam CSS, `workbench_theme.css`. They look right but ship two palettes and are the hardest to maintain.
3. A lot of apparent "old design" is **dead or hidden**: assignments/projects (self-work and coursework switched off, `core/academic_rules.py:40,59`),
   coding exams, the standalone «Mövcud imtahanlar» list (`exam.available_page` default False, `core/runtime_settings.py:354`), the legacy exam-cohort pages,
   posts/blog (owner: "later"). These are listed in section 4 so nobody redesigns them by accident.
4. Native `<select>` is essentially solved (17 bare `<select>` hits, all hidden backing selects or template fragments) — no work package needed.
5. Mobile: no `@media` at all in `journal_student.css` (student journal), `teacher_check_attempt/_part1+_part2.css`, `take_exam/_part1.css`, `course_members.css`.

## 3. Notes on the top rows

* **#1/#2 (student results).** These are the only screens every student sees after every exam, and both leave the cabinet shell (own container, own back
  button). The `exam_result` question-analysis part (`.result-question-card`) is dense and can keep its structure; replace the header block, status
  badge, alerts and the previous-attempts table. `student_exam_history` is the cheapest big win (S).
* **#3 (student journal).** `journal_student.css` is a mock-up palette (`--s-ink`, `--s-mid`, `--s-line`, `--s-cell` … hex, l.5) defined locally; replacing it with `--ems-*` also
  removes the dependency on `jd2.css` for the student page. Check at 375 px: the only responsive rule is inherited from `jd2.css` (`@media (max-width:900px)` l.331, 454).
* **#6 (exam detail).** Two of the three Bootstrap modals (`#examDetailDeleteConfirmModal`, `#examDetailTrialRunBlockedModal`) are plain confirms →
  `_dialog`/`_reason_dialog`. The add/edit-question flow (`?question_modal=`) is the structural problem: it re-renders the whole detail page behind a modal
  (`apps/exams/views/teacher/exams/list_detail.py:357-368`).
* **#10 (taking an exam).** Treat as a *skin* task. Timer, draft-sync, proctoring and the id-card step (`take_exam/*.js`, `exam_supervision/*`) are out of scope for a
  visual pass; PERF-2 and the proctoring tests already depend on their DOM ids.
* **Do not redesign twice:** `my_courses.css` (`.mc-card`) is used by teacher `my-courses` *and* student `assigned-courses`; `assigned_exams.css` is loaded by the
  course dashboard (`apps/courses/templates/courses/course_dashboard.html:32`); `teacher_proctoring_summary.css` is shared with the excluded «Bax» page.
